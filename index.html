🚨 Emergency Instructions

Contact primary vet first if possible. For after-hours emergencies, go to 24-Hour Emergency Vet. Please call us if there are any emergencies. Teddy gets anxious at the vet, so a muzzle may be needed.

📝 Behavioral Notes

Teddy is generally calm but may become anxious in emergency situations. He responds well to gentle voice commands. If he is hesitant to do something that you need him to do, singing him a gentle song may encourage him to be brave.

☀️ Daily Care

⌄

🍽️ Food and Water

Blue Buffalo Senior - Chicken or Salmon Recipes

Morning - 1.5 cup
Evening - 1.5 cup

One Cosequin supplement once per meal

Teddy likes his water fresh - please change bowl daily.

🦴 Treats & Restrictions

Only dental treats allowed. No chocolate, grapes, onions, raisins, or xylitol.

🏃 Exercise & Activities

2 walks daily (30 mins each)

Tug-of-war, fetch, cuddles

🐕 Personality

Traits: Friendly, playful, loves attention, sometimes stubborn, wary of strangers, can be intimidating

Loves: Belly rubs, playtime, walks
Dislikes: Loud noises, being alone, unstable objects

💊 Conditions & Medications

Mild arthritis in hips

Cosequin supplement once per day with a meal

🛏️ Comfort Items

Blanket. Allowed in bed when invited

✨ Quirks

He tends to hide treats around the house.

Thank you for taking care of our precious Teddy! 🐶
